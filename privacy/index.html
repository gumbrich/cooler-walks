Privacy by design.

You can plan a walk without an account. Your route is calculated on your device, using public map data.

Policy draft reviewed 29 September 2026 · Website, Android & iOS

Location is your choice

Cooler Walks requests location only when you tap “Use my location.” The coordinates become your route’s starting point. They are used on your device, are not sent to our website or map-data host, and are not saved as journey history. We do not track you in the background. Your operating system or browser may use its own location-service providers.

You can enter a place or select the map instead. You can revoke location access in your device or browser settings.

What stays on your device

The app remembers your city, selected area, units and whether you have opened the planner. It stores downloaded maps and routing data for offline use. The website may also cache app files. There are no advertising cookies, analytics SDKs, accounts or saved precise journey histories in this version.

Remove saved areas from “Download areas.” Clear the website’s stored data in your browser settings to remove its preferences and caches. For the native app, clear app data or uninstall it to remove local data.

When you connect

Opening the website, updating it or downloading an area contacts the hosting provider. That provider receives normal request information such as IP address, requested file, timestamp and browser information. An area-file request can reveal which city data you download, but does not contain your selected start, destination or route.

External source links open their providers’ websites, whose own privacy notices apply.

When you contact us

If you send feedback by email, we receive the address and information you choose to send so we can reply and investigate. Please leave out precise locations or personal details unless they are necessary for your report. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence.

Publisher & contact


Questions about privacy or your data can be sent to the contact above. We will update this notice before changing how the app handles personal information.
