Help & feedback

Include your city, device and what happened. There’s no need to send your precise location. Feedback is sent by email, not posted publicly.

Where can I walk?

Zurich, Munich, Cambridge (Massachusetts, USA) and Basel have real routing data in the marked pilot areas. The map’s coverage boundary shows where planning is supported. Zurich, Munich and Basel cover selected districts; Cambridge follows its municipal boundary. None of these pilots guarantees complete path or environmental data.

What does “Estimated shade” mean?

Zurich and Munich estimate direct sunlight blocked by buildings for your chosen departure time. Tree proximity is a separate greenery indicator. Cambridge and Basel offer greenery and waterside preferences without shade estimates.

Tree shadows, clouds and terrain shadows are not modelled. This is not a temperature forecast or safety assessment. Choose “Prefer sun” when you would like a sunnier walk; the app does not assume that shade is always your goal.

How do calmer streets work?

In Zurich, choose “Prefer calmer streets” to reduce walking near mapped main roads within your detour limit. Official pedestrian, Begegnungszone and Tempo-30 zones, plus separate paths, provide additional street context. Sidewalks beside main roads also count as main-road proximity. Speed limits do not establish low traffic: this mode has no live vehicle counts, noise measurements or safety rating. It is experimental and limited to the Zurich pilot.

Can I switch language?

Use EN / DE in the header. English and German share the same map data and current trip; local place names, route time zone and your units stay unchanged.

Can I use it offline?

Download an available area while online, wait for “Saved,” then use that area. The app includes or caches some areas depending on the platform. Offline files describe the data at preparation time; they do not include live closures or weather. Removing a download frees its storage; an already open walk may remain visible until you leave it.

Why isn’t there another route?

Sometimes the walking network has no useful alternative within your extra-time limit. Try another start or destination, or allow five or ten extra minutes. If the route crosses unsupported data, the app explains the missing coverage instead of inventing a shade score.

Something isn’t working

Check the selected coverage area and your connection, then retry. A failed download can be retried without deleting a previously saved copy. If location is unavailable, choose a point on the map or enter a place. Outdoor conditions and access can change; follow signs and use your judgment.
